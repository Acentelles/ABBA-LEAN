# ABBA: verification status of the paper's results

Final status as of 2026-08-21. Lean project: `~/dev/abba-lean` (Lean 4.33, Mathlib v4.33, 28 modules, 22 commits `cb84b9f` through `b5d7aa0`, each a green `lake build` with zero `sorry`). Sage battery: `abba_checks.sage`, `abba_comsis_checks.sage`, `abba_uniformity_checks.sage`, and the distributional check, all passing. Caveat: Lean verifies the statements as transcribed into `AbbaLean/*.lean`; the correspondence with the LaTeX statements is by inspection. "Numerical (exact)" means exhaustive or exact-rational computation, not sampling.

**Verification found two problems, both now fixed in `PQCrypto.tex`.** (1) The $m=1$ Proposition and Theorem `Theorem:m=1` were false; the cited commuting-probability formula is the zero-product probability (Dolžan 2022 computes $\Pr[ab=0]$, not $\Pr[ab=ba]$); see `m1-commuting-probability-fix.md`. (2) The proof of Cor `commprobunif` (and hence `Dcollprobthm`, `UnifDistrCor`) had a gap: linearly independent Gaussian samples need not generate $\Lambda_q$ modulo $q$; replaced by a centralizer criterion plus GPV smoothing modulo $\mathfrak{q}\Lambda$, at the cost of the hypothesis $\sigma\geq\eta^{(q)}_\varepsilon(\Lambda)$; see `mgg1-uniformity-fix.md`.
